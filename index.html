Chain Crew — Privacy Policy

Developer: Enclop  ·  Package: com.enclop.chaincrew  ·  Effective: 4 September 2026

This policy covers the Chain Crew mobile game ("the Game", Google Play package com.enclop.chaincrew), published by Enclop ("we", "us"). It explains what the Game collects, who it is shared with, and how to get it deleted. Enclop's company privacy policy covers our website and client services and does not apply to the Game.

The short version

  • Chain Crew is free and funded by ads. Ad networks use your device advertising ID.
  • There is no email or password. Your account is your Google Play Games sign-in.
  • We never see your card. Every payment is handled by Google Play.
  • You can delete everything we hold, from inside the Game or from the web.
  • Chain Crew is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Who we are

Enclop, India. Questions, requests and complaints: enclop.app@gmail.com. We reply to privacy requests within 30 days.

Advertising, and who gets your data

Ads in Chain Crew are served through Yodo1 MAS, a mediation platform that fills each ad slot from a pool of ad networks. Those networks — not Enclop — receive your device's advertising ID and coarse device and ad-interaction data, and they process it as independent controllers under their own privacy policies. They may use it to personalise ads, measure them, and prevent fraud.

The networks that can appear in Chain Crew are:

  • AppLovin — applovin.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob / Ad Manager — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Unity Ads and ironSource — unity.com/legal
  • Meta Audience Network — facebook.com/about/privacy
  • Liftoff / Vungle, Mintegral, Moloco, Pangle, InMobi, Fyber (DT), BidMachine, Bigo Ads, TopOn, Yandex Ads and YSO Network

Yodo1's own policy is at yodo1.com/en/privacy-policy. The list above reflects the ad partners built into the current version of the Game and changes when the Game is updated.

Your choices about ads

  • In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent before any personalised ad is served, using Google's certified consent form. Refusing means you still see ads, but non-personalised ones.
  • You can change that answer at any time: Settings ▸ Legal & Privacy ▸ Ad Consent.
  • Anywhere in the world you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Ads, which stops it being used to build a profile of you.
  • If you are in a US state with an opt-out right, email enclop.app@gmail.com with the subject "Do not sell or share" and we will pass the signal to our ad partners.

Voice chat

Chain Crew includes optional in-game voice chat. Voice is transmitted live between players during a match and is processed only in memory for as long as needed to deliver it in real time. We do not record, store, or listen to voice chat, and it is never saved to any server or device.

Microphone access is optional. The Game asks for your permission before using the microphone, you may decline, and you can mute your microphone at any time from the in-game controls or revoke the permission in your device settings. Declining does not prevent you from playing.

Purchases

All in-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see or receive your card number, bank details, or billing address. We receive only a confirmation from Google that a purchase was made, which we verify so the item can be unlocked for you. Refunds are handled by Google Play — see Google Play refunds.

Children

Chain Crew is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age where you live), and it is not distributed under Google Play's Designed for Families programme. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children, and the Game is configured to tell our ad partners that its audience is not child-directed. If you believe a child has given us data, email enclop.app@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Legal bases (EEA/UK)

  • Performance of a contract — running your account, matches, friends and purchases.
  • Consent — personalised advertising, withdrawable at any time from Settings ▸ Legal & Privacy ▸ Ad Consent.
  • Legitimate interests — security, anti-cheat, abuse prevention, and diagnosing crashes.
  • Legal obligation — keeping purchase and tax records.

How long we keep it

  • Your profile, friends and items: until you delete your data, or after 24 months with no sign-in.
  • Gameplay events: 14 months.
  • Purchase receipts: 8 years, as tax law requires, stripped of anything identifying you.
  • Moderation and ban records: 3 years, so a ban cannot be escaped by deleting and starting over.

Deleting your data

Two routes, both free:

  • In the Game: Settings ▸ Legal & Privacy ▸ Delete My Data. Immediate.
  • On the web, including after uninstalling: enclop.com/chaincrew/delete-my-data, or email enclop.app@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data".

Deletion erases your profile, display name, username, friends, device links and item ownership, and releases your username back into the pool. Your Google Play purchase history belongs to your Google account and is not ours to delete — if you play again on the same account, your items are restored.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, delete, port, or restrict the processing of your data, to object to it, and to withdraw consent. Email enclop.app@gmail.com — we do not charge for this and we do not degrade the Game for people who exercise these rights. EEA/UK players may also complain to their local data protection authority.

Security and transfers

All traffic between the Game and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS, and access to the database is restricted and logged. Our servers and our staff are in India, and our ad partners operate globally, so your data is transferred internationally; those transfers rely on the standard contractual clauses or the equivalent safeguard for your region.

Changes

We will update this page when the Game changes, and the date at the top will change with it. If a change materially affects your rights we will say so in the Game before it takes effect.
